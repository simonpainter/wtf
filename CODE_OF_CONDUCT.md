# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- being respectful and constructive
- accepting and acting on feedback gracefully
- focusing on what is best for the project and community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, discrimination, or hateful language
- trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- public or private intimidation

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please open a private security report as described in `SECURITY.md`.
